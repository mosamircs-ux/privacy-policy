Lumina Files Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-19. This policy covers the Lumina Files app for Android (package com.luminafiles.filemanager).

Lumina Files runs on your device and does not need an account. Your files stay on your phone. The only data that leaves the device is ad-related data sent to Google, described in section 3.

1. Who we are

Lumina Files is an Android file manager based on open-source software (Fossify File Manager) licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Contact details are in section 11.

2. Information the app uses on your device

The app reads and changes the files you ask it to handle: browsing, copying, moving, renaming, deleting, compressing, extracting and editing text files. To do this it asks for access to storage (All files access on Android 11 and later). File contents are processed on your device.

The app stores these settings locally in its private storage:

  • Display and sorting preferences, view type and column count
  • Favorites, home folder and visible tabs
  • Hidden-file visibility and editor text zoom
  • Root access preference, if you turn it on
  • Protection settings for hidden items or the app, if you set them

3. Ads and Google

Lumina Files shows ads from Google AdMob. To request ads, the app sends network requests to Google. Google may use identifiers such as your device's advertising ID, and information about the ads shown, to deliver and measure ads.

Where required by law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or change your ad choices from the About screen (Ad Privacy Settings), when that option is shown, and in your Android settings.

Google explains its handling of data in its Privacy Policy and its ads policies.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number is required.
  • File names, file contents and folder listings are not sent to us.
  • The app's own code includes no analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Root access

If you turn on root access, the app can run commands with root privileges so it can list and manage files that are otherwise protected. These commands run on your device. Their results are not sent to us.

6. Sharing

We do not share your information, except with Google for ads as described in section 3, or when the law requires it.

7. Security and storage

The app's settings are kept in its private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Uninstalling the app removes those settings. Files you manage stay where they are.

8. Children

Lumina Files is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices

  • Revoke storage access in Android settings. The app then cannot browse or change your files.
  • Change ad choices in the About screen or in your Google settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove it and its local settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
