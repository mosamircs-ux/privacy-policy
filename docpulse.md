# Privacy Policy for DocPulse

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

DocPulse ("the app", "we", "our") is a document reader, organizer, and editor for Android, published under the package name `com.docpulse.reader`. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, what it stores, and what is shared when you use it.

## Summary

- DocPulse works on your device. Your documents are opened, read, and edited locally.
- We do not operate servers that collect your documents, files, or personal account information.
- The app has no user accounts and no sign-in.
- The app shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and related information as described below.

## Information the App Accesses

**Documents you open or create.** When you open a file, DocPulse reads it so it can display it. When you edit a file, changes are saved back to the location you chose. Files are selected through the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework), so the app only gets access to the files you choose. The app does not upload your documents to any server operated by us.

**Document metadata stored on your device.** To provide features such as folders, recent items, favorites or bookmarks, search, sorting, and filtering, the app stores information such as file names, locations, file types, and your preferences (for example theme and sort order) in local app storage on your device.

**Network access.** The app requests the Internet and network state permissions. Internet access is used to load advertisements and to open web links you tap in a document. We do not use it to send your documents or personal files to us.

**Advertising ID.** The app declares the Android advertising ID permission (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`) so that Google AdMob can serve ads.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

DocPulse displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use:

- Your Android advertising ID
- Device information such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Information about ads you view and interact with

This information is used by Google to show ads, limit ad frequency, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized advertising, in your Android device settings (Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

Where required by applicable law, consent for personalized advertising is requested through the consent mechanisms provided by Google.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your documents with third parties. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data the app stores (preferences, folder and favorite information) remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can delete the app's data from Android Settings > Apps > DocPulse > Storage > Clear storage. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Your document files themselves are never deleted by uninstalling DocPulse; they remain where they were stored.

Android's system backup may include app data according to your device's backup settings. You can manage backups in your Google account and device settings.

## Security

Because DocPulse stores data locally and does not transmit your documents, the main protection for your files is your device's security (screen lock, encryption, and Android permission controls). We take reasonable steps to protect the app, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

DocPulse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Choices and Rights

- You can deny or revoke storage and other permissions in Android settings. Some features may stop working if you do.
- You can control personalized advertising through your Android device's ad settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. For information processed by Google AdMob, contact Google directly through its privacy resources above. For any other request, contact us as described below.

## Changes to This Policy
