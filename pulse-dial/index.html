Privacy Policy for Pulse Dial

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pulse Dial ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application on Android.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

Pulse Dial is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. Pulse Dial functions as a modern phone dialer and call manager. We do not run private backend servers to collect, store, sell, or monetize your personal calling data, contacts, or call history.

2. Data Handled Locally on Your Device

To provide core telephony functionality, the app requests standard Android system permissions. The following data is processed strictly locally on your device:

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS): Used solely to display your contact list, perform T9 name search from the dialpad, display contact names for incoming/outgoing calls, and allow you to add or edit contacts. Your contacts are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • Call Log (READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG): Used solely to display your recent incoming, outgoing, and missed calls within the app's Recents tab, group call history, and allow you to clear or export your call log.
  • Phone & Calls (CALL_PHONE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS): Required to initiate outgoing phone calls, manage active calls, switch audio routes, and receive incoming call notifications.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Pulse Dial integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on the main navigation tabs. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device-identifying data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and general device information (such as model, OS version, and network carrier).
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Crash reports and ad load performance metrics.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Retention and Security

All personal phone and contact data remains strictly stored in your device's local Android SQLite databases and secure application storage. You can delete your call history, clear application data, or revoke permissions at any time through Android System Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Pulse Dial is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and our target audience on Google Play is set to 18 and older.

Pulse Dial is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3), ensuring full transparency of our code and privacy mechanisms.
